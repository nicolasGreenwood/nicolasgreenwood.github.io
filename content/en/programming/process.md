## THE PROCESS:
{.grid-col-1-5 .highlight-black .animate-box}

**01. Get in Touch**
Send me an email at [nicolas.greenwood@outlook.com](mailto:nicolas.greenwood@outlook.com) with a brief explanation of what kind of services you need. I'll respond to you in 48 hours or less.
{.animate-text}

**02. Find Your Solution**
Together we'll break down exactly what you need and tailor a solution that works for you. I can assist you with any technical decisions you're unsure about as well as sketch out prototypes that match your brand vision. I offer consultations via email and/or video call.
{.animate-text}

**03. The First Build**
When the planning stage is complete, I will start working on an initial version of your project. This can take anywhere from a few days to a few weeks, depending on how large/complex your project is. As soon as it's finished, I'll present you with the completed product.
{.animate-text}

**04. Testing**
At this stage, you will have the opportunity to test the initial version to make sure everything works perfectly and suits your needs. If you notice any issues or have any extra requests, I'll make sure to modify the site accordingly and offer it to you for testing again. If everything looks good, I'll guide you through the deployment process to get your brand new site live and running on the web!
{.animate-text}
