Since relocating to Europe, I’ve had many, many more opportunities to visit these places—some of them old as the 13th century. Standing inside one of these places feels like what I imagine the afterlife would be like: surrounded on all sides with with endless, deep, Byzantine blue as shining faces watch me intently with their anagogic gaze.'

I look down at
bread in a golden bowl,
the flesh of man (or is it the Flesh of God?)
the scent warm and biting
and slightly intoxicating.

I watch wine soak this bread
drench it, drown it
like a wound fountaining
the blood of man (or is it the Blood of God?)
until the bottle empties
and the blood dries out.

I chew bread slowly
as a man’s eyes on the wall behold me
(or are they the Eyes of God?)
and inhale the sharpness
of burning frankincense.
