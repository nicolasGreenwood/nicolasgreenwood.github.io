about_this_poem: Strangers leave a deeper impression on me than I often admit. Sometimes I can recall a completely random face I only ever saw once for years afterwards. I’ve always wondered about the explanation for this. This poem describes this experience, and not without a tinge of melancholy.
---

I see faces I will not see again
faces cold and empty, faces made of stone
and smiles, and a deep, hidden scar
flashing in tender, grief-stricken eyes.

I see faces I will not see again
and these faces I dream of, but can never remember;
I see outlines and smudges of crimson and pink
and the dark shadows of coats crossing the street.
