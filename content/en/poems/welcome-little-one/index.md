*Dear Little One,*

*It is with pleasure that we welcome you to this world. There will be many things you will not understand, but you can trust us to provide you with answers. Over time, you will learn to be productive and happy. We look forward to a long life of cooperation and contribution.*

*Best wishes,
[blank]*

---

Welcome, Little One.

This air you will breathe
These hands you will trust
This name will be yours.
Wear these clothes you must.

This book you will learn
These lines you must say
This path you must take
These words will you pray.

This face you will love
This name you must hate
This work will you do
You must never be late.

In this place you will be
For this flag you must die
This blood you must shed.
In this grave you will lie.
