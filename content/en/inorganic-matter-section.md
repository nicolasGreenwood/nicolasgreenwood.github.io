## New Book: Inorganic Matter

*Inorganic Matter* is my first complete poetry collection. With 43 poems in total, it contains over 30 new poems exclusive to the collection and dedicated commentary for each one.

>*"You are bliss, and immortal; unconscious, like nature.
Chemistry, and the bitter salt in my tears.
A rearrangement of atoms so sweet, so lovely;
you are breath and life, and inorganic matter."*
