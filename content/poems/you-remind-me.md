---
title: You Remind Me
date: 2025-03-03
draft: false
author: Nicolas Greenwood
---

{.poetry}
- You remind me of a time
- When I smiled with tears on my cheeks
- When you graced me with your presence
- When I dipped my fingers into the stream.
{.poetry}
- You remind me of a time
- When the wind was cool
- When it blew your hair
- And covered your eyes.
{.poetry}
- You remind me of a time
- When I gazed at the clouds
- My back to the earth
- And saw your face in them.
{.poetry}
