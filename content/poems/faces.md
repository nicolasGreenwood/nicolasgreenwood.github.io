title: Faces
date: 2025-06-10
draft: false
author: Nicolas Greenwood
---

- On thirsty ground our footsteps fall
- Through barren sand our fingers trace
- Faces with untempered grace
- Untouched by the impossible.
{.poetry}
- A thousand times the words we've said
- Do we mean them is the question
- Do words come before connection? 
- To love conceive must we pretend?
{.poetry}
- Or are our hearts set on fire
- On their own; can a flame ignite
- Without a spark; do souls unite
- Truly by their own desire?
