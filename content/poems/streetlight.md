{.poetry}
- In silence it glows
- In her hand a rose
- From her gaze she knows
- We shall glimpse no more the sun.
{.poetry}
- In darkness complete
- In bitterness sweet
- Our tears on concrete
- We listen to the echo of our own laughter.
{.poetry}
- That was long ago
- Before the sun’s glow
- Left the skies we know
- And left us alone in streetlight.
{.poetry}
- Your hands touch my face
- A tear to erase
- Your finger’s soft trace
- Draws a line through a coat of ashes.
{.poetry}
- Hear the glow of night
- See the frozen light
- Of a silv’ry white
- Moon that listens to our smallest whispers.
{.poetry}
- In our arms to die
- Beneath a dark sky
- Is our destiny
- So it seems, so it shall be.
{.poetry}
- So to my eyes raise
- Your heartbroken gaze
- To see through the haze
- Of a fading streetlight that you wither not in solitude.
{.poetry}
