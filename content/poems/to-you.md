---
title: To You
date: 2025-03-03
draft: false
author: Nicolas Greenwood
---

{.poetry}
- All my life I have dreamed of you
- I’ve seen your shadow in the rain
- Your face in water’s reflection
- Dancing just out of my vision
- Disappearing when I awake.
{.poetry}
- Don’t leave me now, but don’t come too close
- Stay there forever, above me
- Where I can feel your sweet presence
- Just enough to sweeten my days.
{.poetry}
- Yes, you make the sun shine brighter
- You make my tears all the hotter
- You make the rain ever colder
- Until I’m dripping with passion
- With my heart alive in my chest
- Gliding away into the sky
- Feeling motion with my eyes closed
- And spinning delirium.
{.poetry}
- Stay in my imagination
- Don’t touch my face or kiss my lips
- Lest you fade from my dreams for good
- And you lose the magic sparkle
- Of your two ever-fading eyes.
{.poetry}
