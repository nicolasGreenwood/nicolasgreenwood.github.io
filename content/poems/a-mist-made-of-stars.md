---
title: A Mist Made of Stars
date: 2025-05-01
draft: true
author: Nicolas Greenwood
---

Silence, and the snow is blue.

My breath becomes clouds, becomes nothing;
The sky is black, and the trees are dark and still
So motionless, like a picture on the wall
Barely visible, barely there.

Silence, and the snow is blue.

This night is colder than the night before
And in a moonless sky stars shine brighter;
In a tearless eye the light is clearer
but in a tearful one light refracts and disperses
and reflects and refracts again
until a rainbow appears.

Silence, and the snow is blue.

To lie on the ground is to lie on a planet
