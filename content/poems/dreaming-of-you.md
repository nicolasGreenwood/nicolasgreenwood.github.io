---
title: Dreaming of You
date: 2025-06-18
draft: false
author: Nicolas Greenwood
---

{.poetry}
- *Moths dance in the streetlight as*
- *You lean on my shoulder. Shadows play*
- *On a brick wall and*
- *We are silent.*
- *We do not think, but watch*
- *Moonlit leaves wave*
- *And listen to the hum of night*
- *Against the foreground of each other’s breathing.*
{.poetry}
- And though the clouds have covered
- The stars I know from view
- Though the words you whispered
- Are no longer true
- I still say them to myself
- And the dream the voice is you
- *And dream the voice is you.*
{.poetry}
- *We are standing on a bridge*
- *The sunlight is white and strange*
- *Your face is marble and stone*
- *Marble and stone—and the wind*
- *Blows through us as we stand*
- *From a great height over a river*
- *I once dropped a ring into*
- *A ring for you.*
{.poetry}
- And though a hand has covered
- The eyes I know from view
- Though the words I whispered
- Are no longer true
- I still say them to myself
- And dream I speak to you
- *And dream I speak to you.*
