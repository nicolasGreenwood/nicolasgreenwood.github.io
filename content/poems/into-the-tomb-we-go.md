- With gentle footsteps and cold wind
- biting the tips of our tear-stained fingers
- under a sky darker than midnight 
- and our every heartbeat haunted
- by the shadow our bodies leave behind
- in the cave, in the lanternlight
- and with the dread of the existing
- beholding the nonexisting and the
- awful delight we take in the glittering stone
- of the cave wall, shaped like a massive, laughing skull
- and with the torches we grip in perspiring hands
- above the coffin, polished wood reflecting orange
- and the cold, dry, lifeless air
- we fill our lungs so cautiously with
{.poetry}
- into the tomb we go.
{.poetry}
- That air, so clear, so blue
- floating above the sand
- —a sand that has never seen rain
- a sand fine and motionless
- a sand soon to swallow a man's bones—
- that air, we fear
- As pure and as undefiled as moondust
- still and thick
- cold, cruel, merciless!
- A peaceful mantle for the dead.
- Wading through this air
- swimming, breathing it, suffocating on it!
{.poetry}
- —into the tomb we go.
{.poetry}
- And there, 
- in that shallow hollow scratched out in the ground
- a box descends
- a box with man (or is it a man any longer?)
- wrapped in cloth
- his eyes closed, his limbs distorted
- a body, a vessel of thought, 
- of pain, 
- of hot breath
- of a lifetime of memory
- of hands that felt
- of eyes that see
- now to fall back 
- into the ground, into a scratched out hollow
- gaping like a soft, earthen mouth
- to slip between lips of sand
- for those lips to close
- for those hands and
- eyes and
- all that ever was this man
- to fade and crumble and melt and dissolve!
- To become one with that sand
- And that bitter, sterile air—
{.poetry}
- out of the tomb we go.
{.poetry}
- And out into that frigid air
- wet and rotten
- consoling and alive
- we walk, we scatter
- under clouds and sun and a million birds
- quiet or not
- laughing or distraught
- left and right, up and down
- and through the country and the town
- forgetting and remembering and then choosing to forget
- the gaping mouth and those lips of sand
- and that lifeless cave and ghostly air
- and that they and I and even her—
{.poetry}
- into the tomb we go.
