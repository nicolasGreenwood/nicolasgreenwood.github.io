Dear Pacific,
{.poetry}
- You covered my face with freshness
- 'Twas inside the roar I felt your balm
- Of foaming brine washed ashore
- With celestial reflection from the sky
- Dancing below me in silvery grey.
{.poetry}
- I watched your waves leap at the clouds
- Beneath the surface a depth untold
- You hid with an ominous mantle
- Of shimmer bright and waves hypnotic
- A forgotten tale below the light of day.
{.poetry}
- Beneath your ripples of molten lead
- I glimpsed a world of iridescent blue
- A place where tears are never shed
- That fades into darkness’s oblivion
- Like the sky’s night that fades away.
{.poetry}
- I saw palm trees bent in the wind
- A wind that made the grains of sand dance
- Lo, the sky wept; I saw her tears fall
- The rain lent tears to my windswept cheeks
- Your angry waves swirled into the bay.
{.poetry}
- Then, like a dream, it was all gone
- The moonlight reflected off your ripples
- You whispered something I couldn’t understand
- Something about love, something about hate
- Something about a face that slipped away.
{.poetry}
- Ah, tell me, mysterious waves
- The secrets you hold beneath your foam
- The sights you saw, the ages that passed you
- Tell me a story of your origins
- Of the times before night and day.
{.poetry}
- Did you freeze into an icy stone
- When you traversed the cosmic hall
- Glittering with the light of stars
- All to one day wash these humble shores
- And splash these cliffs with glorious spray?
{.poetry}
- And what of tomorrow, dear ocean
- Whither lies your next destination?
- What alien sky will you reflect?
- What foreign shores will you carve and sculpt
- Oh beautiful ocean, so blue, so grey?
{.poetry}
- I understand it now; I hear your voice;
- The answer lies in that strange place
- Where the stars and your surface meet
- Called the horizon by a few of us
- Where begins and ends night and day.
{.poetry}
