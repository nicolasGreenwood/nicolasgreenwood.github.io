This is a film about Saint Sava, a Serbian prince and Orthodox monk who became the first Archbishop of the autocephalous Serbian Church. Known as the Enlightener of Serbia, he was an educator, writer, diplomat and lawmaker and is remembered as one of the greatest establishers of Serbian Orthodox identity.

He is venerated by the Eastern Orthodox Church on January 27, and this film was made as a school project for the occasion. 

You can read more about Saint Sava on [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Saint-Sava).

Here's an English version of the film's narration.

*Saint Sava*

*He was born in 1175, as the son of the great founder of the Nemanjic dynasty, Stefan Nemanja.*

*Leaving behind all the comforts and honor of a prince, as a youth he left home to pray on the shores of Mount Athos.*

*There he became a holy man, there he grew into the spiritual father of his nation.*

*He was also a peacemaker, a symbol of love and unity.*

*He taught his people not only to read, but also to understand the meaning of peace, love and true Orthodox life.*

*He was the first and greatest Serbian enlightener, and his legacy survives to this day and will remain forever.*
